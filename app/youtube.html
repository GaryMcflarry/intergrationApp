integrationAppYouTube Music

The widget has a YouTube Music tab beside Spotify, in the same layout: Now (YouTube's player as the cover), For you (your liked songs, and YouTube's own mixes started from them), Playlists and Search. Starting YouTube pauses Spotify. Sign in once here: your library is kept by the app.

1 · Your Google app

  1. Open the Google Cloud console and create a project (any name).
  2. In APIs & Services → Library, find YouTube Data API v3 and press Enable.
  3. In Google Auth Platform (the OAuth consent screen), choose External, fill in the app name and your email, and under Audience → Test users add your own Google account.
  4. In Clients, create a client of type Web application. Under Authorized JavaScript origins, add exactly:
  5. Copy the client's Client ID (it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) into the box below. It is not a secret.

Google accepts https addresses, or http://localhost / http://127.0.0.1 with the port when trying it locally. While the app is in testing, Google shows a "not verified" warning for your own account: press Continue.

2 · Sign in and bring in your library not signed in

Read-only: the app sees your playlists and liked videos, and cannot change anything. The sign-in lasts an hour and stays in this browser; your library (playlist names, song ids and titles) is kept by the app. Press Sign in again any time to bring in new playlists, or use Refresh library in the card.

YouTube does not give apps your home recommendations. For you uses YouTube's own mixes instead, started from songs you like; they are personal when you are signed in to YouTube in the same browser.

If you use the Worlds wallpapers too, the same Google client works for both: add this address beside the Worlds one.
