The widget shows what is playing on your Spotify account and controls it: Now, Queue, Search, Library (playlists open so you can pick a song inside, with Play and Shuffle) and Devices. Each song's ⛶ button opens the full view, and the DJ button starts DJ mode, which keeps adding new songs in the vibe you pick. Sign in once here.

1 · Your Spotify app

2 · Sign in not signed in

    Signed in before some features were added: press Sign in again so the widget may use all of them.

    If liking songs or adding to playlists fails, press Check Spotify: it lists what your sign-in is allowed to do and Spotify's own answer for each. Showing what is playing works on any account. The buttons (play, skip, seek, volume, queue, moving between devices) need Spotify Premium; that is Spotify's rule.

    If you use the Worlds wallpapers too, the same Spotify app works for both: add this redirect address beside the Worlds one.
