integrationAppSet up your island

integrationApp lives at the top of your screen as a small black island. It shows what is playing on Spotify or YouTube Music (or Discord, when nothing plays). Hover or click it for the three tabs and what is playing; drag it down (or press ⌄) for the full player or your Discord channels; press ⛶ to cover the whole screen. Pin keeps it open; Esc or dragging up closes it.
