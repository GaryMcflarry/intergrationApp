Astrum · set upYouTube

YouTube plays in your browser, and the island shows what plays, its queue and YouTube's suggestions, and controls it, like Spotify. Connect your browser first; a Google app (optional) adds search and your playlists and liked videos, with read-only access.

1 · Connect your browser

  1. In Chrome, Edge or Brave, open the extensions page (chrome://extensions), turn on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and choose the extension/youtube folder in Astrum's folder.
  2. In Astrum, open the YouTube tab and press Copy pairing code. Paste it into the extension's popup (its icon in the toolbar) and press Save.
  3. Play something on youtube.com (reload tabs that were already open). It shows up in the island.

2 · Make a Google app (optional)

  1. Open the Google Cloud console and create a project (any name).
  2. In APIs & Services → Library, find YouTube Data API v3 and press Enable.
  3. In Google Auth Platform (the OAuth consent screen), choose External, fill in the app name and your email, and under Audience → Test users add your Google account.
