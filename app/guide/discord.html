Astrum · set upDiscord

Three things, each optional: show what you play on your Discord profile, see the latest messages from your servers in the island, and share a song to a channel. All three start with one free Discord application of your own.

1 · Your Discord application

  1. Open the Discord developer portal and press New Application. Its name is what Discord shows as “Listening to …”, so call it something like Music.
  2. On General Information, copy the Application ID here.

Rich Presence goes through the Discord app on this computer, so keep it running.

2 · Messages in the island

This server feed uses a bot of yours to read the channels you pick. It does not connect your personal conversations, friends list, or calls. Those require a separate authorized Discord integration.

  1. In your application, open Bot. Under Privileged Gateway Intents, switch on Message Content Intent (so it can see what messages say) and save.
  2. Press Reset Token, copy the token and paste it below. Keep it secret: the app stores it encrypted and never shows it again.
  3. Add the bot to your server: invite it (enter the Application ID above first). It only asks to view channels and read their history.

3 · Share a song to a channel

  1. In Discord, open a channel's Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook, then Copy Webhook URL.
  2. Paste it here. The island's Share to Discord buttons then post the song there.
