Discord · Rich Presence …

While music plays, your Discord profile shows Listening to the song and artist, with the cover, how far along it is, and a button to open it. It needs the Discord app running on this computer, and a Discord application of your own (its name is what Discord shows, so call it something like Music).

  1. Open the Discord developer portal and press New Application. Any name.
  2. On its General Information page, copy the Application ID into the box below.

Discord · Share to a channel

The Share to Discord buttons (in the player's Now band and in the full view) post the song to a channel of your choice.

  1. In Discord: the channel's Edit Channel (or Server Settings) → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook.
  2. Press Copy Webhook URL and paste it below. Keep it to yourself: anyone with it can post there.
